Glossary

Terms used throughout the dashboard
Term Meaning
Top risk / Bottom opportunity How close an asset looks to a major top or bottom, 0 to 100. Two separate readings, never combined, because tops and bottoms behave differently — bottoms are sharp, tops are slow.
Broad A reading built from every indicator on that asset, weighted equally, whether or not any of them has a real track record. A summary of what the indicators collectively say, not proof that any of them works.
Validated A reading built only from indicators that passed a decades-long backtest on that specific asset. Reads "none" wherever nothing has earned its place yet — true for most assets, most weeks.
Evidence tier — Strong / Moderate / Thin How much to trust a specific claim. Strong: a validated indicator with a stable, sizeable track record. Moderate: validated, but resting on a thin sample or unstable since 2015. Thin: no validated indicator at all — most of the board, most weeks.
Elevated / moderate / low / none How extreme a broad reading is, in plain words: none at 0, low above 0, moderate from 25, elevated from 50, very high from 75.
Flags in a zone How many of an asset's own indicators are currently flagging a top or bottom condition, out of how many are tracked for it.
Firings How many separate times, across all of market history, an indicator has entered its zone — its sample size. Fewer than 5 and it is never scored at all.
Edge How much better, or worse, an indicator's signal performed against what that asset typically does over the same period, in percentage points.
Out of sample The same edge, measured only on data since 2015 — after the indicator's rule was written. Confirms the edge is not an artefact of the exact history it was built on.

Evidence tiers

The one scale that decides how much to trust a claim, used everywhere on the dashboard instead of comparing raw scores across assets.

What Strong, Moderate and Thin mean, and what to do with each
Tier What it takes to earn it What to do with it
Strong Admitted, stable out of sample, and resting on at least 8 independent past cases. Backed by a real track record — worth a closer look.
Moderate Admitted, but either resting on a thinner sample (5-7 cases) or flagged unstable — its edge weakened or reversed after 2015. Some track record behind it, but thinner than ideal — worth a look, held loosely.
Thin No indicator has passed the backtest on this asset and side at all. The ordinary case. No proven signal here — shown for completeness, not something to act on.
