# Market Cycles Dashboard

A weekly research dashboard estimating, for 30 major asset classes (in both US
dollars and Indian rupees), how close conditions are to a major **top zone** or
**bottom zone** — using only indicators that have been validated against decades
of their own history.

**Live page:** https://radcan1.github.io/market-cycles-dashboard/

## What this is

- **Portfolio-independent by design.** It holds no information about any
  holdings, allocation, or investment policy. Inputs are public market data
  only. It answers "where is this asset in its cycle?" — never "what should be
  bought or sold."
- **Validated, not folkloric.** Every indicator was event-studied on decades of
  history for each asset; those that failed are published as rejected. The full
  scorecard is in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).
- **Two gauges, never one.** Top risk and bottom opportunity are scored
  separately, because tops are slow rolling processes and bottoms are sharp
  fear events.

## The one-line finding

Signals that measure panic *as it happens* (fear spikes, deep cyclical
drawdowns, capitulation) carry information; signals that try to *predict* the
cycle in advance (the yield curve, sentiment surveys, most macro forecasts) do
not.

## Caveats

This is research, not investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee
future results; several findings rest on few historical events. The dashboard
regenerates weekly.
