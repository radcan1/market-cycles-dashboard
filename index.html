Method and caveats

This page estimates how close each asset class is to conditions that have historically accompanied major tops and bottoms. It does not predict prices, and it says nothing about any portfolio: it holds no information about holdings, allocations or investment policy, by design.

Tops and bottoms behave differently — bottoms tend to be sharp, fear-driven events while tops are long rolling processes — so the two gauges are scored separately and never combined into a single verdict.

On this page every indicator is equally weighted, so a composite is a summary of what the indicators collectively say, not a weighted verdict. The validation engine has separately measured each indicator's real hit rate on each asset across decades of history and recorded which earned their place and which were rejected; those results live in the validation scorecard. Equal weighting is kept deliberately here rather than promoting the handful of admitted signals, because most weeks the honest reading is context, not a call.

Full method: see the design specification and the implementation specification in the docs folder of this project.
